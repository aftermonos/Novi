# Contributing

Thank you for your interest in Novi.

The project is in its earliest phase, focused on character and interaction design. There is no codebase open for contributions yet, so the most helpful thing right now is feedback.

## Ways to help

- **Share how you review your trades.** What do you write down, what do you forget, and what do you wish you could ask later?
- **React to the concepts.** If something in the character or interface designs stands out, good or bad, we want to hear it.
- **Suggest ideas.** Features, behaviors, or moments where a companion would genuinely help.

Open an issue using the **Feedback** or **Idea** template to get started.

## Ground rules

- Be kind and specific.
- Keep discussion focused on the product and the experience.
- Please do not post trade recommendations or promotional content.
