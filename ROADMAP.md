# Roadmap

Novi is being built in four phases. Each phase has a clear goal and a short list of outcomes. Scope will change as we learn, and this page will be updated when it does.

## 01 Identity

**Status: in progress**

Define who Novi is before writing the product around it.

- Character design, turnarounds, and expression set
- Palette and visual language for the app and brand
- Interaction concepts for the desktop window
- Early motion and rigging studies
- Tone of voice for how Novi speaks

## 02 Prototype

**Status: planned**

A first working version of the companion on the desktop.

- A small, persistent desktop window
- Text conversation with Novi
- Saving trade notes and reasoning from conversation
- Basic character animation tied to conversation state

## 03 Memory

**Status: planned**

Turn individual conversations into a shared history.

- Remembering user preferences across sessions
- Recalling past reasoning on request
- Gentle reflection on recurring habits
- Voice input
- Clear controls for viewing and deleting what Novi remembers

## 04 Early access

**Status: planned**

Put Novi in front of real traders and listen.

- Private testing with a small group
- Feedback loops on tone, memory, and usefulness
- Refinement of character behavior and motion

## Principles that guide every phase

- **A thinking partner, not a signal service.** Novi is not intended to recommend trades.
- **Your history belongs to you.** Users should always be able to see and remove what Novi remembers.
- **Calm by default.** Novi should never compete with your charts for attention.
